Create example workload manifests, wire them into the existing spoke trees with

<threat_model>
**ASVS Level:** 1
**Block on:** high

- **T-05-04 Secret leakage in public manifests:** Example manifests must not
  contain tokens, kubeconfigs, passwords, private hostnames, or user-specific
  values. Mitigation: only public podinfo and NVIDIA CUDA image references are
  committed.
- **T-05-05 GitOps drift:** Imperative workload apply to spokes can hide Flux
  failures. Mitigation: `deploy-examples` is GitOps-only and static tests reject
  direct spoke `kubectl apply`.
- **T-05-06 Excessive GPU rerun mutation:** Deleting broad resources could remove
  Phase 4 seed state. Mitigation: rerun deletion is limited to
  `gpu-smoke/nvidia-smi` Job only.
</threat_model>
